All figures show development results on one shared 200-device ensemble. Lines connect budget levels within the same optimizer seed. Circles/squares/triangles identify seeds 7500/7501/7502. Diamonds and bars show means and min–max over three seeds, not device confidence intervals. Empty cells are unfinished, never zero. Pareto lines are descriptive across unequal costs and are not a matched-efficiency frontier. The leakage acceptance line applies only to maximum leakage. The 5% realized-cost comparability rule is a descriptive analysis rule introduced after three low-call results were observed, not a prespecified statistical hypothesis.
