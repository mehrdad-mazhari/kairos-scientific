# Results summary

Exploratory: 27 original trials reached the shared iteration cap before resource caps, producing identical waveforms across nominal matching regimes. Corrected: 54 separate trials enforced call, scenario-pass and wall budgets; all remained budget-limited and none certified convergence. Cross-resource costs are not interchangeable.

Confirmatory: 2,000 fixed devices, five frozen pulses, 10,000 CPU production evaluations. Six paired device-index bootstrap comparisons use 50,000 resamples and Bonferroni simultaneous intervals; 100,000-resample sensitivity is reported separately. Conventional and continuation beat DRAG; fixed risk beats DRAG modestly but is inferior to conventional. Continuation beats fixed risk. Continuation versus conventional is inconclusive. Four primary engineering certificates pass. Diagnostic-only unfiltered risk fails sampled leakage. Full favorable, unfavorable and inconclusive outcomes are retained.

See `../results/confirmatory/` and authoritative JSON/checkpoints under `../reproducibility/original/results/confirmatory_validation_v1/`. Numerical verification, P05 certificates, cost accounting, development comparisons and figures are included there. No campaign was regenerated for this release.
